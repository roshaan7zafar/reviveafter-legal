## Managing your subscription

Subscriptions are handled by Apple and Google, so you manage or cancel them in your store account rather than in the app.

**iPhone:** Settings → your name → Subscriptions → ReviveAfter Pro.

**Android:** Google Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → ReviveAfter Pro.

Cancelling stops the next renewal. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for.

## Restoring a purchase

If you've reinstalled the app or switched phones, open the paywall in ReviveAfter and tap **Restore purchases**. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you subscribed with.

## Your data

Your matches, check-ins and plans are tied to your ReviveAfter account. See the [Privacy Policy](https://roshaan7zafar.github.io/reviveafter-legal/privacy) for what's stored and why.
