# Privacy Policy

## ReviveAfter

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This policy explains what ReviveAfter collects, why, and what happens to it. It describes the app as it actually works rather than covering things we might do one day.

ReviveAfter is run by **Roshaan Zafar**, an individual developer, who is the data controller for the purposes of the UK and EU General Data Protection Regulation.

Contact for any privacy question or request: **roshaanzafar619@gmail.com**

## What we collect
### Your account

When you create an account we store your **email address** and a securely hashed password. We never store your password in a readable form.

### What you tell us during setup

Onboarding asks four optional questions. You can skip any of them:

- Bodyweight in kilograms — used to calculate your hydration target. Skipping it means we assume 75 kg.
- How often you play
- Your biggest recovery struggle
- Which days and time of day you usually play — used to schedule the reminder to log a match

### Matches you log

For each match: the date, whether it was singles or doubles, duration, intensity, how hot it was, how tired you felt beforehand, and any body areas you flagged as sore.

### Evening check-ins

How tired you feel that evening on a 1–5 scale, optionally how you slept, and optionally whether you followed the plan.

### Recovery plans

The plan generated for each match is stored against that match so you can read it again later.

### On your device only

Notification scheduling and whether you responded to a reminder are stored on your phone and never sent to us.

### What we do not collect

No location data. No contacts. No photos. No advertising identifiers. No health data from Apple Health or Google Fit. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

## Why we collect it

**To generate your recovery plan**. Your bodyweight and the details of the match you logged determine the hydration, fuel, and cooldown guidance you receive.

**To show you your own trend**. Your check-ins over time produce the fatigue chart and the weekly observation.

**To remind you**. Your play schedule determines when your phone reminds you to log a match.

**To manage your subscription**. We record whether your account is on a free trial, subscribed, or expired.

Our lawful basis under GDPR is **performance of a contract** — we cannot provide the app's function without this information. You choose what to tell us, and the app works with less.

## Who else sees it

**Supabase** hosts the database and handles account authentication. Your data is stored on their infrastructure. They act as our processor and do not use your data for their own purposes.

**Anthropic** receives the details of a logged match — duration, intensity, heat, soreness, and the calculated hydration and cooldown figures — in order to write the plan text. **Your email address and name are never sent**. Anthropic does not train models on this data.

**RevenueCat**, together with **Apple** or **Google**, handles subscriptions. They receive your account identifier (not your email address) and your purchase status. Payment card details go directly to Apple or Google and never reach us.

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers. We have no analytics SDK in the app.

## How long we keep it

Your data is kept while your account exists. If you ask us to delete your account we remove your profile, all logged matches, all check-ins, and all generated plans. Deletion is permanent and we cannot recover it afterwards.

Email roshaanzafar619@gmail.com to request deletion. We will action it within 30 days.

## Your rights

Under GDPR and similar laws you can ask us to:

- **Give you a copy** of everything we hold about you
- **Correct** anything inaccurate
- **Delete** your account and all its data
- **Restrict or object** to how we use it
- **Export** your data in a portable format

Email **roshaanzafar619@gmail.com** and we will respond within 30 days. There is no charge.

If you are in the UK or EU and are unhappy with our response, you can complain to your national data protection authority.

## Where your data is stored

Our database is hosted in Singapore. If you use ReviveAfter from the UK or EU, your data is transferred outside your region. We rely on the standard contractual clauses in our agreement with Supabase to protect it.

## Health information

ReviveAfter records how tired you feel and your bodyweight. This is wellness information, not medical information, and the app does not diagnose, treat, or give medical advice — as the disclaimer in the app says on every plan.

We treat this data with the same care as anything else here: it is never sold, never shared with advertisers, and used only to generate your plans and your trend.

## Children

ReviveAfter is not intended for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

## Security

Passwords are hashed. All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit. Database access is restricted by row-level security, so your rows are readable only by your own account.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the relevant authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of it.

## Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your data, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Significant changes will be notified in the app.
