# Delete your ReviveAfter account

In the app: open ReviveAfter → Settings → Delete account, and confirm. Your account and all your data are deleted immediately.

If you can't access the app, email roshaanzafar619@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, and we'll delete it within 30 days.


## What gets deleted

- Your account and email address
- Your profile: bodyweight, play frequency, recovery struggle, play schedule
- Every match you have logged
- Every evening check-in
- Every recovery plan generated for you

Deletion is permanent. We cannot recover it afterwards.

## What we keep

Nothing. Once your account is deleted we hold no data about you.

Your subscription is managed by Apple or Google rather than by us — cancel it in your device's subscription settings. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription.
